## Executive summary

Caterpillar reported sales and revenues of $17.4 billion, up 22.2% year over year, while operating profit increased 19.6%. Construction Industries was the fastest-growing segment, with revenue up 38.1%. [Income statements][S1] [Segment disclosures][S2]

## Investor takeaways

- **Operating performance:** Operating profit grew more slowly than sales and revenues (19.6% versus 22.2%). [Income statements][S1]
- **Sales volume and pricing:** Caterpillar attributes most of the $3.166 billion increase in sales and revenues, to $17.415 billion, to volume (+$2.3 billion) and pricing (+$426 million). [Management discussion][S4]
