## Earnings basis

The table uses GAAP diluted EPS of $5.47 for Q1 2026 and $4.20 for Q1 2025. Caterpillar also reports adjusted non-GAAP profit per share of $5.54 and $4.25, respectively. The disclosed restructuring costs were $41 million ($0.07 per share) in 2026 and $33 million ($0.05 per share) in 2025; the adjusted measure excludes these items. See Management discussion below.

## Segment reporting basis

The 2025 segment figures were retrospectively adjusted to the 2026 reporting structure. From January 1, 2026, responsibility for diesel-electric, hybrid and battery-electric locomotives and related rail products moved from Power & Energy to Resource Industries. The comparison therefore uses the revised segment basis. See Segment reporting basis below.
