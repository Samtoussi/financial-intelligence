## Earnings basis

The table uses GAAP earnings. Microsoft also reports adjusted net income and adjusted diluted EPS that exclude net losses from investments in OpenAI; those non-GAAP measures are reconciled to GAAP in the filing. See Management discussion below.

## Segment reporting basis

For the periods shown, Microsoft reports three segments: Productivity and Business Processes, Intelligent Cloud, and More Personal Computing. Management and the chief operating decision maker review segment profit-and-loss information and use operating income to assess performance and allocate resources. See Segment reporting basis below (Note 16).
