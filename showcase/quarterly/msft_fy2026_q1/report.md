## Executive summary

Microsoft reported revenue of $77.7 billion, up 18.4% year over year, while operating income increased 24.3%. Intelligent Cloud was the fastest-growing segment, with revenue up 28.2%. [Income statements][S1] [Segment disclosures][S2]

## Investor takeaways

- **Operating performance:** Operating income grew faster than revenue (24.3% versus 18.4%). [Income statements][S1]
- **Cloud growth and margin:** Microsoft’s MD&A associates Intelligent Cloud growth with Azure. It reports that Microsoft Cloud’s gross margin percentage fell to 68%, reflecting AI infrastructure expansion and greater use of AI features despite efficiency improvements in Azure and Microsoft 365 Commercial cloud.
