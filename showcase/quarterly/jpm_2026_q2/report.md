## Reported versus managed context

Reported results are the Firm’s U.S. GAAP consolidated financial statements; managed (FTE) basis reclassifies tax‑exempt and tax‑credit items to be comparable to taxable items but does not change net income. For Q2, FTE adjustments increased total net revenue by $675 million to a managed total of $58,022 million (noninterest revenue +$564 million; net interest income +$111 million); the Firm states these adjustments have no impact on reported net income.
